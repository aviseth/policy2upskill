Data contract v0.1.0 (proposal, not yet agreed)

Skill, demand and indicator values are synthetic; employee, population and completion figures are real (Statistics Denmark). Skill rows are per municipality, occupation and skill. Demand rows are per municipality and occupation, with region and national comparators.

FieldRule
municipalityLowercase slug from the municipalities list
occupation, skillLabels, free text
categoryKind of change to the skill: rising, falling, durable, transforming
trendDirection of demand for this skill in this occupation: rising, falling, level
confidence0 to 1. Below 0.5 shows as low confidence
scenarioTrue for a what-if assumption, never observed data
sourcesNon-empty list. Scenario rows start with "Scenario:"; other rows name their planned sources
index_municipality, index_region, index_nationalDemand index, 2019 = 100, range 1 to 150
