# Demo click path (Danish, about 5 minutes)
1. Open `prototype/index.html`. It opens on Lolland at Municipality level. Point at the first panel: past years solid (real, Statistics Denmark), 2026-28 dashed, "trend extension, not a forecast".
2. Compare panel: Lolland with Frederiksberg. Employees, change, population and completions are real (green). Skill and shortage counts are synthetic (amber).
3. Scroll to "Skills by occupation". Expand the heat-pump row, then tick "Show scenario signals": the Femern row appears, marked "assumption".
4. Click Region: the Sjælland heat-grid (Lolland, Guldborgsund, Næstved), with real employees above it.
5. Click National: the league table of occupations, with real All Denmark employees above it.
6. Click Alerts in the top bar (rule-based, synthetic). Click a municipality tile to return to the explorer.
Say: totals are real; skills, demand index, shortage flags and time to fill are synthetic; nothing here is a forecast.
